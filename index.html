Frequently Asked Questions

What is SAP SD?

SAP SD (Sales and Distribution) manages the complete order-to-cash process: inquiry, quotation, sales order, delivery, billing and payment.

Who can join the SAP S/4 HANA SD course in Vijayawada?

Yes. SD integrates with MM, FI/CO, PP and WM, and the course covers these links.
